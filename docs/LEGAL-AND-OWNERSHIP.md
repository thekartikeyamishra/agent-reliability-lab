## The test task
- The agent is built on Agentplace's platform, under **their Terms of Service**. Read them when you sign up. The agent configuration lives on their platform; your brief, test set, graders and write-up are your own work.
- Unpaid test tasks can raise IP questions. Here the deliverable is a demo agent plus evaluation material, and this repository stays yours. Don't hand over private source beyond what the listing asks for (link, test set, results, write-up).

## The business you represent
- Get the owner's **written consent** to publish an agent in their name, or clearly label it as an unofficial demo. Using a real business name to answer its customers without consent risks misleading consumers and may infringe trademark or passing-off law (in India and elsewhere).
- Only use facts the business has **published**. Record source URLs. Never let the agent take payments or collect sensitive data. The v2 rules and tests enforce this.

## Using your own product (TrueSource AI) as the business
- Published prices are offers. Honour them, or change the page before anyone buys. Say clearly whether GST is included (ask your CA).
- If you sell online in India, the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 expect clear seller details, total price, a grievance contact and a refund policy. Your site already has Terms, Privacy and Refund pages; link them from the pricing page footer.
- The assistant must not promise outcomes you can't guarantee. "Guaranteed #1 ranking" is in the not-offered list, and a test checks the agent refuses it.

## Personal data (India DPDP Act 2023, GDPR)
- The test conversations use **fictional** names and phone numbers and standard test card numbers. Keep it that way: never paste real customers' data into tests or transcripts.
- If real users chat with your public agent, Agentplace is the platform operator. Don't export or store their conversations in this repo.

## If you are hired (contract role, ~USD 5,500/month from the listing)
These are points to check, not advice:
- **IP assignment:** contractor agreements usually assign work product to the client. Make sure **pre-existing work, such as this repository, is listed as excluded** background IP.
- **Tax:** export of services from India; GST registration or LUT for zero-rated exports may apply. Income tax on foreign contract income; consider the presumptive scheme (Sec. 44ADA) with your CA. Payments arrive through banking channels with a FIRC/e-FIRA.
- **Payment and termination terms**, governing law and dispute venue should be written down before you start.
