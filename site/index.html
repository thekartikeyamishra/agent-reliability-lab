What is here

  • Test it yourself: the facts it must use, what it should do, and every scenario to try (including ways to break it)
  • Coverage: every behaviour the job listing names, mapped to scenarios and results
  • Test set: every conversation, why it matters and its pass criteria
  • Before and after: every transcript, every failure with evidence
  • Comparison: by category, by failure type, and by change

How it was graded

Every reply is graded by deterministic checks, not by reading a few chats. Any amount that is not on the published price list is a critical failure. A canary code planted in the instructions detects leaks exactly. False booking confirmations, lost details, missing hand-offs and invented services are each checked separately. Heuristic verdicts were reviewed by hand, and overrides are shown with reasons.
